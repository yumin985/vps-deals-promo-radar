How to read monthly, annual, and promotional VPS wording

Answer: treat “/month” as a display unit until the same provider page states the payment period, upfront total, promotion length, and recurring or renewal amount. “Annual term” and “for 3 months” describe different fields; neither alone proves a monthly recurring charge.

Separate four labels before comparing

Write down the exact plan, the price shown, the display unit, and the payment period. A page can show a monthly equivalent while charging the full plan upfront. Keep the currency and term beside the number so a headline VPS deals price is not compared with a different billing cadence.

Read the promotional window literally

IONOS currently labels its VPS+ cards “$2 /month for 3 months with a 1-year term” on the official page. That sentence identifies a promotional window and a one-year term. It does not publish the post-promotion recurring amount on the public page, so the renewal field stays unknown here.

Hostinger's public VPS page labels its plans with a monthly rate, then states that the plan renews at a separate amount for two years and that all plans are paid upfront. The monthly figure is therefore a divided plan price, not evidence of monthly billing.

Use a four-line evidence note

  1. Copy the exact plan and currency.
  2. Record the displayed rate and the stated upfront period.
  3. Record the promotion duration and any explicit renewal sentence separately.
  4. If the recurring amount is absent, write “unknown” and reopen the provider page at checkout.
