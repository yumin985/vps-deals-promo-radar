How to compare a VPS deal when tax appears only at checkout

Answer: compare the advertised rate, the stated billing term, the pre-tax subtotal, and the checkout tax line as separate fields. A public note that taxes may apply is not a customer-specific tax amount. Keep the final amount unknown until checkout shows the currency, tax or fee lines, and total charged now.

Capture the public offer first

Copy the plan name, currency, display rate, payment term, and promotion window exactly as the provider shows them. A “/month” label can be a comparison unit for a prepaid term. Hostinger states that its VPS plans are paid upfront and that the monthly rate is divided from the total, so the display rate is not automatically the amount charged each month.

Mark tax as a checkout-only field

IONOS's public VPS page says state and local sales taxes may apply. That is evidence that tax can change the total, but it does not publish a universal customer tax amount. Record tax unknown until the checkout summary shows the line for the buyer's location and currency.

Compare like with like

  1. Display rate: advertised number, unit, and currency.
  2. Term and subtotal: period covered and amount due before tax or fees.
  3. Tax and fees: every checkout line item, or “unknown” when not exposed.
  4. Final total: amount charged now, in the same currency.

Do not subtract or add an assumed percentage. Do not treat a missing tax line as zero. If two providers use different terms or one shows tax only at checkout, pause the comparison until the same fields are visible.
