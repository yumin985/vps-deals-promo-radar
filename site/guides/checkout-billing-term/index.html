How to verify a VPS billing term at checkout

Answer: before paying, confirm that checkout shows the same plan, currency, first billing period, and recurring charge as the provider's public VPS page. If the recurring term is absent, leave it unknown and stop before placing the order.

Capture four fields before you click pay

  1. Plan identity: match the exact plan name and resource tier. A checkout upgrade or add-on can change the term you are checking.
  2. First term: record the displayed amount, currency, and period, such as monthly, annual, or a stated three-month promotion. Do not convert a longer term into a monthly figure unless the provider labels it that way.
  3. Renewal wording: look for “renews at,” “recurring,” or “after the initial term.” Copy the provider's amount exactly; a percentage discount alone does not establish the resulting renewal price.
  4. Evidence: save the official source URL and UTC fetched-at time next to your checkout note. Terms can change between a landing page and checkout.
