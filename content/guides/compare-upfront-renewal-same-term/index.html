VPS deals reader question

How do I compare a VPS upfront payment and renewal rate without mixing terms?

Answer: compare an upfront payment with a renewal rate only after the plan, currency, and billing term match. A provider can show a 24-month payment today and a monthly renewal rate for that same 24-month term; those are different fields, not competing monthly prices. If any label does not match, leave the comparison unresolved.

Use a four-label match before calculating

1. PlanExact tier and configuration
2. CurrencySame currency on both labels
3. Payment nowAmount and term it covers
4. RenewalAmount, unit, and renewal term

Do not compare a renewal figure for one VPS tier with a promotion for another, and do not assume that a “/mo” label means monthly billing. A prepaid multi-month plan can still use a monthly rate as a comparison unit.

What the public Hostinger page shows

Public-source evidence — checked 2026-10-29T01:05:02Z

Hostinger VPS hosting visibly states: “Today you pay $$155.76 for 24 months. Renews at $$11.99/mo for the same term.” The page also says all plans are paid upfront and that its monthly rate reflects the total plan price divided by the number of months in the plan.

This supports a term-aware comparison for that labelled card only. It does not establish the buyer's tax, checkout total, or the renewal price of a different plan or term.

Read the two amounts correctly

  1. Upfront payment: $$155.76 is the stated amount due for the stated 24-month first term.
  2. Renewal rate: $$11.99/mo is explicitly labelled as renewal for the same term, so retain both its unit and term beside it.
  3. Do not flatten labels: dividing, annualizing, or comparing to another plan can be useful only after the provider's exact terms remain aligned. It must not replace the provider's labels.

IONOS's public VPS page was also checked at the same time. Its offer cards identify a 3-month promotion, but the collected fields do not expose a verified post-promotion renewal amount. That missing field stays unknown; it is not filled from Hostinger or inferred from a discount.

Decision rule: an upfront amount and renewal rate are comparable evidence only when the provider connects both to the same plan, currency, and term. Tax, fees, checkout total, and any unlabelled renewal amount remain unknown until the provider exposes them.
