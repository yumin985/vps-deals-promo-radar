# 2026-10-18 T2 competitor scan

Query: `"VPS deal" renewal pricing checkout` (Google, 2026-10-18 Asia/Shanghai). I opened each of the ten first-page results.

## Excluded
- racknerd-renewal-pricing on GitHub: same subject, but GitHub is a giant platform rather than a focused competitor.
- HostAdvice: accessible and relevant, but a broad high-authority web-hosting publisher; excluded as a wall rather than a direct peer.
- Medium and LinkedIn: giant platforms; LinkedIn result was unavailable content.
- LM Owners ProBoards: browser safety gate blocked access, so it was excluded.
- CloudSurph: accessible, but primarily a $1 VPS provider list; renewal is a brief pitfall rather than the page's question.
- TradingVPSHub: accessible and relevant to Hostinger renewal, but mainly a trader-specific provider page; held out for a narrower future comparison.

## Final lock after owner review
- toolrelief.com — `VPS Renewal Cost Calculator: Promo vs Renewal` directly models promo price, renewal rate, add-ons, and ownership horizon using dated vendor evidence. It is the only retained peer.

BearHost was excluded as a provider's own commercial site; Lumafare was excluded because its domain has no validated traffic signal. No wording was copied. Our gap remains the lack of a compact calculator or explicit renewal comparison table; our differentiator is source URL plus fetched_at evidence and the unknown-field rule.
