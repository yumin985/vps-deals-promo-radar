How to read a VPS deal when renewal wording is missing

Answer: treat the renewal amount as unknown until the provider publishes an explicit recurring or post-promotion charge. Save the exact plan, billing period, currency, official URL, and fetched-at time, then verify the checkout terms before paying.

What to capture from the public page

  1. Headline and term: record the plan name, displayed amount, currency, and whether the page says monthly, annual, or “3-month promotion.”
  2. Renewal wording: copy only an explicit recurring, renewal, or “after the initial term” amount. A percentage or a missing sentence does not prove a final price.
  3. Evidence trail: keep the source URL and UTC fetched-at time beside the note. Reopen the same official page at checkout because prices and terms can change.

What today's records establish

The 2026-10-17 collection records four Hostinger plans and five IONOS plans with source URLs and fetched-at timestamps. The IONOS titles say “3-month promotion,” but the dataset has no verified renewal-price field. This page therefore leaves the post-promotion amount unknown.

Do not fill the gap: ask the provider or read the checkout summary for the recurring amount before ordering. Unknown is the accurate value when the official page does not publish it.
