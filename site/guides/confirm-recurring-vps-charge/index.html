How to confirm a recurring VPS charge after a promotion

Answer: find an explicit recurring amount or “renews at” line for the exact plan and billing period at checkout. If the provider shows only a promotional first term, record the recurring charge as unknown and do not infer it from the discount.

Check the same plan at both stages

Copy the exact plan name, resource tier, currency, and first billing period from the public offer. At checkout, confirm that the selected plan has not changed through an upgrade, add-on, or different billing cadence. A first-term price is evidence only for that first term.

Look for the renewal sentence

Search checkout and the provider's published terms for wording such as “renews at,” “recurring,” or “after the initial term.” Record the amount exactly as shown, including currency and period. A percentage discount, “from” price, or annual total does not prove a recurring monthly price.

Keep a source record

  1. Save the official offer or terms URL.
  2. Record the UTC fetched-at time and the plan label.
  3. Write down the first-term amount and the explicitly stated recurring amount separately.
  4. If the recurring field is absent, leave it blank and return to the provider before paying.
